About retr-oq

This is a prototype Windows 7 (Aero) desktop -- a glass taskbar with a real Start orb, and windows you can drag by the title bar and resize from any edge or corner, minimize down to a taskbar button, and maximize to fill the screen.

Built on 7.css -- see this theme's style.css for the Aero glass/gloss decisions made for the title bars and taskbar.
